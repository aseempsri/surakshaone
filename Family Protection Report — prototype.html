How a plan gets offered

Nothing is pitched on the report itself. The offer is made afterwards, on a call, and only where the person's own answers show it applies.

Pricing rules

  • The report is never discounted, because it is already free. Discounting anything under it destroys the anchor.
  • Claim Assist is quoted flat, never hourly. A bereaved family cannot evaluate an hourly rate and will not engage on one.
  • The 10% success fee applies only to assets the family did not know existed. Charging it on a nominated asset they would have received anyway is a regulatory complaint waiting to happen.
  • White-label is priced low enough that the partner treats it as free. We are buying distribution, not recurring revenue.
