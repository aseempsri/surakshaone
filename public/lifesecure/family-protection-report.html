Pricing rules

Guardrails that keep the offer stack trustworthy.

1

Never discount the report

It is already free. Discounting anything under it destroys the price anchor.

2

Claim Assist is flat, never hourly

A bereaved family cannot evaluate an hourly rate and will not engage on one.

3

10% only on unknown assets

The success fee applies only to assets the family did not know existed — never on nominated assets they would have received anyway.

4

White-label buys distribution

Priced low enough that partners treat it as free. We are buying reach, not ARR.
